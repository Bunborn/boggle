2026-04-05 -- Developer Diary
=====================================

Fixed the memory leak in freeBoard() that the test diary flagged. Classic
two-level allocation, one-level free -- buildBoard() allocates each row of
cubes and isVisited individually, but the old freeBoard() only freed the outer
pointer arrays and then called it a day. Valgrind would have had a field day.

The fix is exactly what you'd expect: loop over the rows, free each inner
array first, then free the outer arrays, then free the board struct itself.
Mirror the allocation order in reverse. Five extra lines, no clever tricks.

Resisted the urge to refactor anything else while I was in there. The rest of
buildBoard() and freeBoard() is fine -- no reason to touch it. In and out.

The QA diary also called out fillValidWords doing a shallow pointer copy as a
"ticking time bomb." That one's on the list but it's a different problem for
a different day. One thing at a time.
